About

I am a Computer Science undergraduate at Taylor's University in Malaysia, specialising in Artificial Intelligence with an extension in Data Science. I expect to graduate in February 2028.

Under the supervision of Dr. Soobia Saeed, I am first author of two papers and co-first author of one. One has been accepted at ICCRD 2027 and two are under peer review. They cover a federated-learning system that trains medical-image classifiers without sharing patient data, explainable prediction of seizures after traumatic brain injury, and an analytics framework with dashboards for emergency departments.

I also work part-time as a lab assistant at the Vortex Lab, where I set up and test extended-reality headsets, including Meta Quest and Microsoft HoloLens.

From 2012 to 2024 I competed in badminton, including seven years on Iran's national team. Training at that level taught me the discipline and persistence I now bring to research.

Under review

MediPro 2026

MediPro: A Privacy-Preserving AI-Based Federated Learning System for Neurosurgical Disease Prediction and Clinical Decision Support

Alireza Karami (first author), et al.

Manuscript under peer review, 2026.

Under review First author Federated learning Medical imaging

A clinical decision support system that trains a shared medical-image classifier across hospitals without exchanging patient data. Each simulated hospital fine-tunes a DenseNet121 model on its own images, and only the model weights are combined, using Federated Averaging (FedAvg).

My contribution: first author; proposed the system and designed its architecture (Django back end, MongoDB database, mobile front end).

AiMH 2027

Multimodal Artificial Intelligence for Personalized Seizure Risk Stratification and Clinical Decision Support in Traumatic Brain Injury

Alireza Karami (first author), et al.

Submitted to the 3rd International Conference on AI in Medicine and Healthcare (AiMH 2027), Malé, Maldives.

Under review First author Explainable AI Clinical decision support

An AI clinical decision support system (AI-CDSS) that predicts post-traumatic seizures and clinical outcomes in patients with traumatic brain injury, and explains each prediction so that risk can be stratified for the individual patient.

  1. Multimodal patient data
  2. LR, RF, SVM and XGBoost
  3. Stratified 5-fold CV
  4. SHAP feature attribution
  5. Personalised risk stratification

My contribution: data analysis, model evaluation and manuscript preparation.
